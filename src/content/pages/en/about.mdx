## Who am I

I am a Full Stack Engineer, Academic Researcher, and Leadership Speaker from Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria. Now in my early thirties, my focus is bridging technical development with real-world impact as I actively advance toward doctoral studies in Germany. 

My journey in technology began on August 15, 2011, at the age of sixteen. What started as a challenging personal hobby has evolved into a dedicated career. Without formal courses, I relied entirely on self-directed learning to master my craft. Platforms like YouTube were instrumental to my growth; I acquired foundational web development skills from resources like [Any channel taltking talk](https://www.youtube.com/@toyyhib) and [Any channel shoot tech on](https://www.youtube.com/@braviaprime).

Today, I am advancing my expertise in TypeScript, React.js, Node.js, and database management. I particularly enjoy using [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) to engineer robust, user-centric web applications. To maintain high engineering standards, I host my code repositories on [GitHub](https://github.com) and deploy my websites seamlessly using [Vercel](https://vercel.com).


## About this site

- Framework: [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/)
- Database: [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/)
- ORM: [Drizzle](https://orm.drizzle.team/)
- Authentication: [Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com/)
- Deployment: [Vercel](https://vercel.com)
- Content: [MDX](https://mdxjs.com/)
- Analytics: [Umami](https://github.com/umami-software/umami)
- Styling: [Tailwindcss](https://tailwindcss.com)
- Code syntax highlighting: [Shiki](https://github.com/shikijs/shiki)

Thanks to **Timothy Lin** for the
[tailwind-nextjs-starter-blog](https://github.com/timlrx/tailwind-nextjs-starter-blog). It was helpful to me at a time when I had no understanding of how to build a blog from scratch. Although I initially followed a starter template, my final implementation differs significantly because I revised and modified a substantial portion of the original code. :)
